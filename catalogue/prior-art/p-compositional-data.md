## Purpose

Compositions carry relative information only; centred and isometric log-ratio transforms give the appropriate geometry.

## Content

Aitchison's compositional-data analysis: compositions (vectors constrained to a simplex, summing to one) carry only *relative* information, and the centred and isometric log-ratio transforms give the geometry appropriate to that constraint — equal ratios, not equal differences, are equally significant.

Directly used, not merely background: the centred log-ratio (clr) is the specified coordinate system for the full-predictive probe target in `c-inst-derived` (§9.2 of the original protocol), and its two-part rationale is stated in `c-terminology`. This citation was flagged in the reconciled amendments (R30) as "currently uncited despite §9.2 using it" — i.e. the clr choice was already load-bearing in the design before its source was named. Recorded here to close that gap.
